**Splash Screen (2 seconds)**
- RitualSathi logo
- Auto-transitions to Welcome

**Welcome Screen**
- Hero image/content
- "Get Started" button → Onboarding
- "Login" button → Login screen

**Onboarding Flow**
1. **Select Ceremony** (e.g., "Wedding")
2. **Set Budget** (e.g., ₹2,50,000)
