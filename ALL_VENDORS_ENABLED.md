## How to Browse Vendors

### Method 1: Home Screen Categories
1. Go to **Home** screen
2. Click any category (e.g., "Priest / Pandit")
3. See 6 niches
4. Click a niche (e.g., "Wedding Pandit")

6. Click "View Details" on any vendor

### Method 2: Search & Filter
1. Open niche listing (e.g., Wedding Pandit)
2. Use search bar to find by name/location
3. Filter by:
   - Area (Tollygunge, Salt Lake, etc.)
   - Price range (Budget, Mid, Premium)
4. Sort by:
   - Rating (highest first)
   - Price (lowest first)
   - Distance (nearest first)
